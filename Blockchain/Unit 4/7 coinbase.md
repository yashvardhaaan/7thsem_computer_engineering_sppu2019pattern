## **Topic 7: Coinbase**

### **1. What is Coinbase?**

[Coinbase Logo & Brand Kit - SVG, PNG, Vector - Logo.dev](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/aSfPNx8jLFRZJJTsaEy1FbN_EOS1i8tv49rlkIZaedYhiieUf7Mmf-p-nTsPB-MCpCz_Imq0oGJDqSpwrKFKGcDYnkdeHVXq5GM3QK2rhkbyJBKpkbnk-qzaybGvNmILmTDTcPjj2oXVBJMZJ3HbKwPyL-0v1qzFfxFzh07jBME?purpose=inline)

**Coinbase** is a cryptocurrency exchange platform that allows users to buy, sell, store and manage supported cryptocurrencies.

It provides services through web and mobile applications.

Coinbase is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange. It acts as an intermediary between buyers and sellers.

### **2. Features of Coinbase**

1. **Buy cryptocurrency:** Users can purchase supported cryptocurrencies.
2. **Sell cryptocurrency:** Users can sell their digital assets.
3. **Crypto storage:** Provides hosted wallet services for customer assets.
4. **Portfolio tracking:** Users can monitor their cryptocurrency holdings.
5. **Trading:** Supports buying and selling of various cryptocurrencies.
6. **Security features:** Offers account protection features such as two-step verification.
7. **Mobile application:** Users can access services through its mobile app.

1. **Account creation:** User creates an account on Coinbase.
2. **Verification:** Completes the identity verification required by the platform.
3. **Payment method:** Adds an available payment method.
4. **Buying cryptocurrency:** Selects a cryptocurrency and purchases it.
5. **Holding assets:** Stores supported assets in the Coinbase account or transfers them to a self-custody wallet.
6. **Selling or transferring:** Sells cryptocurrency or transfers it to another wallet.

### **4. Applications of Coinbase**

- Buying and selling cryptocurrencies.
- Managing a cryptocurrency portfolio.
- Sending and receiving supported digital assets.
- Monitoring cryptocurrency prices.
- Accessing supported blockchain-related services.

### **5. Advantages of Coinbase**

1. User-friendly interface.
2. Convenient buying and selling process.
3. Supports multiple cryptocurrencies.
4. Provides account security features.
5. Offers web and mobile access.
6. Provides portfolio management tools.

### **6. Disadvantages of Coinbase**

1. Trading and transaction fees may apply.
2. Availability of services varies by country.
3. A centralized platform involves dependence on the service provider.
4. Account access may be restricted for security or compliance reasons.
5. Users must protect their account credentials against phishing and unauthorized access.

### **7. Coinbase vs Coinbase Wallet**

These are different products:

| Coinbase exchange                                              | Coinbase Wallet                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Centralized exchange platform.                                 | Self-custody wallet.                                  |
| Platform manages custody of assets held in its hosted account. | User controls the private keys or recovery phrase.    |
| Used mainly for buying and selling.                            | Used for managing assets and connecting to dApps.     |
| Account access depends on Coinbase services.                   | User is responsible for wallet recovery and security. |
