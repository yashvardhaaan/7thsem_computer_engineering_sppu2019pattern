# **Unit IV – Cryptocurrency and Token**

## **Topic 5: Cryptocurrency Usage**

Cryptocurrency can be used for transferring digital value, making payments and accessing blockchain-based services.

### **1. Uses of cryptocurrency**

1. **Online payments:** Used to pay for goods and services where cryptocurrency is accepted.
2. **Peer-to-peer transfers:** Allows users to transfer digital currency directly without a traditional payment intermediary.
3. **International remittances:** Can facilitate cross-border transfers, subject to network fees, exchange rates and regulations.
4. **Investment:** Some people purchase cryptocurrencies as speculative or long-term investments.
5. **Decentralized Finance (DeFi):** Used in blockchain-based lending, borrowing and decentralized exchanges.
6. **Fundraising:** Some blockchain projects issue tokens to raise funds, subject to applicable laws.
7. **Smart contracts:** Cryptocurrencies and tokens can interact with programmable blockchain applications.
8. **Digital ownership:** Certain tokens represent ownership, access rights or claims to digital or physical assets.

### **2. Relevance of cryptocurrency usage**

Cryptocurrency usage is relevant because it provides:

- **Decentralization:** Transfers can take place without a single central authority controlling the network.
- **Global accessibility:** Users with internet access can participate in supported cryptocurrency networks.
- **Transparency:** Public blockchain transactions can be independently verified.
- **Programmability:** Tokens can be used with smart contracts and decentralized applications.
- **Financial innovation:** Enables new services such as DeFi and token-based applications.

### **3. Examples of cryptocurrency usage**

| Example           | Usage                                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bitcoin           | Peer-to-peer digital value transfer                               |
| Ether (ETH)       | Paying Ethereum network fees and interacting with smart contracts |
| USDC              | Digital transfers using a stablecoin                              |
| Governance tokens | Voting on decisions in decentralized projects                     |
| Utility tokens    | Accessing services within a blockchain platform                   |

### **4. Limitations and risks**

1. **Price volatility:** Cryptocurrency prices can change rapidly.
2. **Security threats:** Phishing, hacking and wallet theft can cause losses.
3. **Irreversible transactions:** Incorrect transfers may be difficult or impossible to reverse.
4. **Regulatory uncertainty:** Rules and permitted uses vary by country.
5. **Scams:** Fraudulent projects and fake investment schemes can target users.
6. **Scalability:** Some networks experience congestion and high transaction fees.

### **5. Safe usage practices**

- Use trusted wallets and platforms.
- Keep private keys and recovery phrases confidential.
- Verify the receiver's address and blockchain network.
- Enable two-factor authentication where available.
- Avoid suspicious links and guaranteed-return investment schemes.
- Understand the risks and applicable regulations before transacting.
