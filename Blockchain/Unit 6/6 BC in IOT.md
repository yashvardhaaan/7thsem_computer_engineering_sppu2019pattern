# **Unit VI – Blockchain Case Studies**

## **Topic 6: Blockchain in IoT (Internet of Things)**

### **1. Introduction**

The **Internet of Things (IoT)** is a network of physical devices connected to the internet. These devices collect and exchange data using sensors, software, and communication technologies.

Examples include:

- Smart home devices
- Fitness bands and smart watches
- Industrial sensors
- Smart electricity meters
- Connected vehicles

Blockchain can be integrated with IoT to provide secure, transparent, and decentralized data management.

IoT devices generate large amounts of data and communicate with one another. Blockchain can help record transactions, verify device identities, and maintain a trusted history of data exchanges.

### **2. Why Integrate Blockchain with IoT?**

Traditional IoT systems often depend on centralized servers. This can create problems such as:

1. **Single point of failure:** If the central server fails, connected devices may stop communicating.
2. **Security risks:** Unauthorized users may access or manipulate device data.
3. **Data integrity:** It may be difficult to verify whether collected data has been modified.
4. **Scalability:** Managing a large number of connected devices can be challenging.
5. **Trust:** Devices belonging to different organizations may need a common way to verify transactions.

Blockchain can help address these challenges through a distributed ledger and cryptographic verification.

### **3. Applications of Blockchain in IoT**

**1. Smart Homes**

- Smart devices can securely exchange information.
- Blockchain can maintain records of device interactions.
- Smart contracts can automate predefined actions, such as access permissions.

**2. Supply Chain and Logistics**

- IoT sensors track the location and condition of goods.
- Blockchain records the sensor data and movement history.
- Helps improve product traceability.

**3. Smart Cities**

- Connected sensors collect information about traffic, parking, and public utilities.
- Blockchain can provide a shared record of data exchanges.
- Helps improve transparency among participating systems.

**4. Healthcare IoT**

- Wearable devices collect health-related measurements.
- Blockchain can help maintain traceable records and manage authorized data sharing.
- Supports secure exchange of information with healthcare providers.

**5. Industrial IoT**

- Sensors monitor machinery and industrial equipment.
- Blockchain can record maintenance events and machine activity.
- Smart contracts can trigger predefined maintenance or supply processes.

**6. Energy Management**

- Smart meters record electricity consumption.
- Blockchain can support peer-to-peer energy transactions.
- Smart contracts can automate agreed energy exchanges.

### **4. Working of Blockchain with IoT**

## **Blockchain-enabled IoT system**

**IoT Devices and Sensors**

Collect data from the physical environment

**IoT Gateway / Network**

Transfers data to the blockchain-connected system

**Blockchain Network**

Validates and records transactions or data references

**Smart Contracts / Applications**

Execute predefined rules and provide authorized access

**Explanation:**

1. IoT sensors collect data such as temperature, location, or energy consumption.
2. The data is transmitted through an IoT gateway.
3. The blockchain network validates the relevant transaction or data record.
4. The record or its reference is stored in the blockchain.
5. Smart contracts can execute predefined actions based on specified conditions.
6. Authorized users can verify the recorded information.

### **5. Benefits of Blockchain Integration with IoT**

| Benefit          | Explanation                                          |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Decentralization | Reduces dependence on a single central authority.    |
| Security         | Cryptography helps protect transactions and records. |
| Data integrity   | Makes unauthorized modification easier to detect.    |
| Transparency     | Provides a shared and traceable transaction history. |
| Automation       | Smart contracts can automate device interactions.    |
| Trust            | Helps devices and organizations verify transactions. |

### **6. Challenges**

- **Limited resources:** Many IoT devices have limited processing power, memory, and battery capacity.
- **Scalability:** A large number of IoT devices can generate more transactions than some blockchain networks can handle.
- **Latency:** Blockchain validation may introduce delays.
- **Storage:** Storing large volumes of IoT data directly on-chain is impractical.
- **Integration:** Connecting different IoT devices and blockchain platforms can be difficult.

### **7. Example: Smart Electricity Meter**

Consider a smart electricity meter connected to an IoT network.

1. The meter measures electricity consumption.
2. It sends the readings to the connected system.
3. Blockchain records the reading or its verified reference.
4. A smart contract calculates the payment based on predefined rules.
5. The consumer and electricity provider can verify the recorded transaction.

This can improve transparency in electricity billing and energy transactions.
