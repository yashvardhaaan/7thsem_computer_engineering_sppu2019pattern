## **1. What is Proof of Stake?**

**Proof of Stake (PoS)** is a blockchain consensus mechanism in which validators are selected to validate transactions and create new blocks based on the amount of cryptocurrency they lock or *stake* in the network.

Unlike Proof of Work (PoW), PoS does not require miners to solve computationally difficult puzzles. Instead, participants put their cryptocurrency at stake to participate in the validation process.

**Example:** If a participant stakes 100 coins and another stakes 500 coins, the second participant may have a higher chance of being selected as a validator, depending on the protocol's selection rules.

## **2. How does PoS work?**

[Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake Comparison](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/S_43__jjhjFpPUed026g9v2sucOcrH_dtqoo4M7YtvqjnbCTGw6qNKPRb7mJuhfnZgFkS8g-kFPYcEh-61yiWJcrLauKADV7PZCTmB-JeqrlFSvILyDvsXeJvY5mrkU7sFCm0FR_qbBoLiuDS0rvqGtwH338-fpguo6bqE9wscI?purpose=inline)

[changenow.io](https://changenow.io/blog/pow-vs-pos-difference?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

1. **Staking:** Participants lock a specified amount of cryptocurrency in the blockchain network.
2. **Validator selection:** The network selects a validator according to its PoS rules. Stake may influence the selection probability.
3. **Block creation:** The selected validator proposes a new block containing verified transactions.
4. **Block validation:** Other validators check the proposed block and participate in confirming it.
5. **Block addition:** Once the required consensus is achieved, the block is added to the blockchain.
6. **Rewards and penalties:** Honest validators receive rewards. Validators who violate protocol rules may lose some or all of their stake through penalties or slashing.

## **3. Important characteristics**

- **Stake-based selection:** Validators participate by locking cryptocurrency.
- **Energy efficient:** No energy-intensive mining puzzle is required.
- **Security through economic incentives:** Validators risk losing their stake if they act dishonestly.
- **Rewards:** Validators can earn rewards for performing their duties correctly.
- **No traditional mining:** Block creation is performed by validators rather than PoW miners.

## **4. Advantages of PoS**

1. **Low energy consumption:** Requires significantly less computational power than PoW.
2. **Cost-effective:** Does not require specialized mining hardware.
3. **Security incentives:** Dishonest behaviour can result in loss of staked coins.
4. **Scalability potential:** Can support efficient transaction validation.
5. **Accessibility:** Users may participate without purchasing expensive mining equipment, subject to the network's requirements.

## **5. Disadvantages of PoS**

1. **Wealth concentration:** Participants with larger stakes may have greater influence.
2. **Nothing-at-stake concern:** Some PoS designs must address validators supporting competing blockchain histories.
3. **Stake requirements:** Participants need cryptocurrency to become validators.
4. **Complexity:** Validator selection, penalties and reward mechanisms can be complex.
5. **Centralisation risk:** Large staking pools or entities may gain significant control.

## **6. PoW vs PoS – Important comparison**

| Proof of Work (PoW)                            | Proof of Stake (PoS)                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Uses computational power                       | Uses staked cryptocurrency                            |
| Miners compete to solve puzzles                | Validators are selected by protocol rules             |
| High energy consumption                        | Low energy consumption                                |
| Requires mining hardware                       | Requires cryptocurrency stake                         |
| Security depends on cost of computational work | Security is supported by economic stake and penalties |
| Example: Bitcoin                               | Example: Ethereum (after The Merge)                   |

## **1. What is Proof of Elapsed Time?**

**Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET)** is a consensus mechanism in which participating nodes wait for a randomly assigned period of time. The node whose waiting period finishes first gets the opportunity to create the next block.

It is designed to select a block creator fairly while consuming less computational energy than Proof of Work.

PoET is associated with **Intel's Sawtooth (Hyperledger Sawtooth) platform**.

## **2. How does PoET work?**

[Understanding Consensus Mechanisms in Blockchain](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/yCcxRMl8mIFL873vv7L8EoVcWsM3a7VX_1LE65NDmwOytB3G3nMV6t9SB34SBxEfy7l8Ibds4c-mAfVq9gWb6nZJ3MgWz22jLO9a13aY4ZkVURDofsXWyiVLj3KyVA9QnCsdNeGAP7nfJmo2Eq70nYLHAM_SCndsd0KoiwKV1dQ?purpose=inline)

[softobotics.org](https://www.softobotics.org/blogs/consensus-mechanisms-in-blockchain/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

1. **Node registration:** Participating nodes join the blockchain network.
2. **Random time assignment:** Each node receives a randomly assigned waiting time.
3. **Waiting period:** Every node waits for its assigned time.
4. **Winner selection:** The node whose timer expires first wins the opportunity to create a block.
5. **Verification:** Other nodes verify the waiting-time proof.
6. **Block creation:** The winning node creates and broadcasts the block.

## **3. Example**

Suppose three nodes participate in a blockchain network:

| Node   | Assigned waiting time |
| ------ | --------------------- |
| Node A | 15 seconds            |
| Node B | 8 seconds             |
| Node C | 20 seconds            |

Node B has the shortest waiting time, so it gets the opportunity to create the next block, provided its proof is valid.

## **4. Advantages of PoET**

1. **Energy efficient:** Does not require continuous computational puzzle solving.
2. **Low hardware requirements:** Does not need powerful mining equipment.
3. **Fair selection:** Random waiting times help distribute block-creation opportunities.
4. **Scalable design:** Can be suitable for permissioned blockchain networks.
5. **Reduced resource consumption:** Nodes can wait rather than compete through intensive computation.

## **5. Disadvantages of PoET**

1. **Trusted hardware dependency:** Traditional PoET implementations rely on trusted execution environments such as Intel SGX.
2. **Hardware compatibility:** Not every system supports the required trusted execution technology.
3. **Security concerns:** Vulnerabilities in trusted hardware can affect the system.
4. **Limited adoption:** It is less widely used than PoW and PoS.
5. **Verification complexity:** The network must verify that the waiting period was assigned and completed correctly.

## **6. PoET vs PoW**

| PoET                                           | PoW                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Uses randomly assigned waiting time            | Uses computational puzzles   |
| Low energy consumption                         | High energy consumption      |
| Does not require continuous mining computation | Requires mining computation  |
| Traditionally relies on trusted hardware       | Relies on computational work |
| Associated with Hyperledger Sawtooth           | Used by Bitcoin              |
