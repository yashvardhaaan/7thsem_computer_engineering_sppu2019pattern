## **1. What is Proof of Burn?**

**Proof of Burn (PoB)** is a blockchain consensus mechanism in which participants permanently destroy a certain amount of cryptocurrency by sending it to an inaccessible address.

This process is called *burning coins*. By sacrificing cryptocurrency, participants demonstrate commitment to the network and may earn the right to mine or validate blocks, depending on the protocol.

Unlike Proof of Work, PoB does not require continuous energy-intensive mining calculations.

1. **Coin selection:** A participant decides how much cryptocurrency to burn.
2. **Burn transaction:** The participant sends coins to an address from which they cannot be recovered.
3. **Verification:** The blockchain verifies and records the burn transaction.
4. **Proof generation:** The recorded transaction acts as evidence that the participant has sacrificed coins.
5. **Validator or miner selection:** The protocol uses the burn amount or related rules to determine participation rights.
6. **Block creation:** The selected participant gets an opportunity to create or validate blocks.
7. **Rewards:** The participant may receive rewards according to the protocol.

## **3. Example**

Suppose a blockchain participant burns 100 coins by sending them to an inaccessible address.

- The network verifies the burn transaction.
- The 100 coins are permanently removed from circulation.
- The protocol may give the participant mining or validation opportunities based on the amount burned.
- The participant may earn rewards for successfully participating.

**Important:** Burning coins does not guarantee a reward. The exact selection and reward rules depend on the blockchain protocol.

## **4. Advantages of Proof of Burn**

1. **Energy efficient:** Does not require continuous computational mining.
2. **Long-term commitment:** Participants demonstrate commitment by sacrificing cryptocurrency.
3. **Reduced circulating supply:** Burned coins are permanently removed from circulation.
4. **No specialised mining hardware:** Participants do not need powerful mining equipment.
5. **Economic security:** Dishonest participation can make the burned investment economically costly.

## **5. Disadvantages of Proof of Burn**

1. **Permanent financial loss:** Burned coins cannot be recovered.
2. **Wealth advantage:** Participants who can afford to burn more coins may gain greater influence.
3. **Uncertain rewards:** Burning coins does not guarantee future rewards.
4. **Limited adoption:** It is less widely used than PoW and PoS.
5. **Economic risk:** The value of the cryptocurrency may change significantly.

## **7. PoW vs PoS vs PoB**

| Feature              | PoW                             | PoS                   | PoB                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Main mechanism       | Computational work              | Staking coins         | Burning coins                         |
| Energy consumption   | High                            | Low                   | Low                                   |
| Financial commitment | Mining hardware and electricity | Locked cryptocurrency | Permanently sacrificed cryptocurrency |
| Special hardware     | Often needed                    | Not generally needed  | Not generally needed                  |
| Main disadvantage    | Energy consumption              | Stake concentration   | Permanent loss of coins               |
