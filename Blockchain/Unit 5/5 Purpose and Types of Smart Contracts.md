## **Topic 5: Purpose and Types of Smart Contracts**

### **1. Purpose of Smart Contracts**

Smart contracts are designed to automate agreements and transactions on a blockchain. Their purpose is to execute predefined instructions when specified conditions are satisfied.

**Main purposes:**

1. **Automation:** Automatically performs actions when conditions are met.
2. **Removing intermediaries:** Reduces dependence on brokers, agents and other third parties.
3. **Transparency:** Makes contract rules and transactions verifiable.
4. **Security:** Uses cryptographic mechanisms and blockchain validation.
5. **Cost reduction:** Reduces paperwork and manual processing.
6. **Accuracy:** Executes predefined rules without human calculation errors.
7. **Trust:** Allows parties to interact without relying entirely on each other.
8. **Efficiency:** Speeds up transactions and business processes.

### **2. Types of Smart Contracts**

A commonly used classification includes the following three types. The classification can vary depending on the application and textbook.

#### **A. Smart Legal Contracts**

- Digital agreements that represent legally enforceable obligations between parties.
- Contract conditions and obligations are written in code.
- They can automate payments, penalties and other agreed actions.
- They must still satisfy applicable legal requirements to be enforceable.

**Example:** An insurance agreement that automatically processes a payout when an eligible claim is verified.

#### **B. Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)**

- Organizations whose rules and operations are managed through smart contracts.
- Members can participate in decision-making through voting.
- Decisions and transactions are recorded on the blockchain.
- Reduces dependence on centralized management.

**Example:** A decentralized investment organization where members vote on how funds should be used.

#### **C. Application Logic Contracts**

- Smart contracts that contain the business logic of decentralized applications (DApps).
- They define how an application behaves and how users interact with it.
- They can manage transactions, records, permissions and digital assets.

**Example:** A decentralized marketplace smart contract that manages product listings, payments and ownership transfers.

### **3. Comparison of Types**

| Type                       | Main purpose                        | Example                   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Smart Legal Contract       | Automates contractual obligations   | Insurance agreement       |
| DAO                        | Manages decentralized organizations | Community voting          |
| Application Logic Contract | Controls DApp functionality         | Decentralized marketplace |

### **4. Real-world applications**

| Application  | Purpose of smart contract                          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Banking      | Automates financial transactions and settlements.  |
| Insurance    | Processes eligible claims automatically.           |
| Supply chain | Tracks goods and automates payments.               |
| Real estate  | Automates property-related transactions.           |
| Healthcare   | Controls authorized access to records.             |
| Voting       | Records votes and applies predefined voting rules. |
