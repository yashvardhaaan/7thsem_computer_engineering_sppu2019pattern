# 3. OO THEMES

## 1. Introduction

**Object-Oriented Themes** are the fundamental ideas or characteristics that form the basis of object-oriented technology.

They explain how object-oriented systems represent real-world entities, organize software, allow objects to communicate, and manage complexity.

### Exam Definition

> **OO themes are the fundamental concepts and principles of object-oriented technology that describe how objects are identified, structured, related, and interact with each other in a software system.**

---

# 2. Important OO Themes

For examination, the important OO themes are:

1. Abstraction
2. Encapsulation
3. Combining Data and Behavior
4. Sharing
5. Object Identity
6. Communication through Messages
7. Inheritance
8. Polymorphism
9. Classification
10. Hierarchy

The exact naming can vary slightly depending on the textbook, but the concepts are the important part.

---

# 3. Abstraction

## Definition

**Abstraction** means representing the important characteristics of an object while ignoring unnecessary implementation details.

It focuses on:

> **What an object does rather than how it does it.**
