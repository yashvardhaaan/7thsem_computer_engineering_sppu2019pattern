# H. ISO 9000 Standard



SPPU PYQ – May/June 2024

6 Marks



This is a standalone SQA topic. Prepare its definition, objectives, principles, family of standards, requirements and importance in software quality assurance.

## 1. What is ISO 9000?

ISO 9000 is a family of international standards developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) that provides guidelines and fundamentals for establishing and maintaining an effective Quality Management System (QMS).

It helps organisations consistently provide products and services that meet customer requirements and applicable regulatory requirements.

In Software Quality Assurance, ISO 9000 helps software organisations establish systematic quality management processes for software development, testing, delivery and maintenance.

Example: A software development company follows documented procedures for requirement analysis, coding, testing and defect management to maintain consistent software quality.

## 2. Objectives of ISO 9000

1. Improve product and service quality.
2. Increase customer satisfaction.
3. Establish standardised quality management processes.
4. Reduce errors and defects.
5. Improve organisational efficiency.
6. Encourage continuous improvement.
7. Ensure consistency in products and services.

## 3. ISO 9000 Family of Standards

The ISO 9000 family contains several standards related to quality management.

| Standard  | Purpose                                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| ISO 9000  | Fundamentals and vocabulary of Quality Management Systems |
| ISO 9001  | Requirements for establishing a Quality Management System |
| ISO 9004  | Guidance for achieving sustained organisational success   |
| ISO 19011 | Guidelines for auditing management systems                |

Important distinction:

- ISO 9000 explains the fundamentals and terminology.
- ISO 9001 specifies the requirements against which an organisation can be certified.

## 4. Principles of ISO 9000 Quality Management

The ISO 9000 family follows seven quality management principles.

1\. Customer Focus

- Understand customer needs and expectations.
- Focus on delivering customer satisfaction.

2\. Leadership

- Management should establish quality objectives.
- Leaders should create an environment that supports quality improvement.

3\. Engagement of People

- Employees at all levels should participate in quality activities.
- Encourage teamwork and responsibility.

4\. Process Approach

- Manage activities as interconnected processes.
- Improve efficiency through systematic process management.

5\. Improvement

- Continuously improve products, services and processes.
- Identify problems and take corrective actions.

6\. Evidence-Based Decision Making

- Make decisions using data, facts and analysis.
- Use quality metrics to measure performance.

7\. Relationship Management

- Maintain good relationships with suppliers, partners and other interested parties.
- Improve organisational performance through effective coordination.

## 5. Requirements of ISO 9001 in Software Quality Assurance

ISO 9001 provides requirements for a Quality Management System. Important areas include:

1. Context of the Organisation: Understand organisational requirements and interested parties.
2. Leadership: Establish quality policies and responsibilities.
3. Planning: Identify quality risks, opportunities and objectives.
4. Support: Provide resources, training and documentation.
5. Operation: Control processes involved in product and service delivery.
6. Performance Evaluation: Monitor, measure and audit quality performance.
7. Improvement: Correct problems and continuously improve the QMS.

## 6. Implementation of ISO 9000 in Software Organisations

1\. Understand ISO 9001 Requirements

2\. Establish Quality Management System

3\. Document Processes and Procedures

4\. Implement Quality Activities

5\. Conduct Internal Audits

6\. Correct Non-Conformities

7\. Certification Audit (if pursued)

8\. Continuous Improvement

For example, a software company can implement documented coding standards, testing procedures, code reviews, defect tracking and release management to maintain consistent software quality.

## 7. Advantages of ISO 9000

1. Improves software quality and consistency.
2. Increases customer confidence and satisfaction.
3. Reduces defects and process-related errors.
4. Improves organisational efficiency.
5. Establishes clear responsibilities and procedures.
6. Supports continuous improvement.
7. Improves credibility and market reputation.

## 8. Conclusion

ISO 9000 is an international family of quality management standards that helps organisations establish and maintain effective Quality Management Systems. In Software Quality Assurance, it supports standardised processes, customer satisfaction, defect reduction and continuous improvement. ISO 9001 provides the requirements used for QMS certification.
