Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is a broad field of computer science concerned with creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, and decision-making.

Examples
Voice assistants
Autonomous vehicles
Expert systems
AI chatbots
Game-playing systems
Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance without requiring every rule to be explicitly programmed.

Examples
Spam detection
Recommendation systems
Fraud detection
Image classification
House-price prediction

Therefore:

AI is the broader concept, while Machine Learning is one of the important techniques used to achieve AI.

Comparison of AI and ML
Basis	Artificial Intelligence	Machine Learning
Meaning	Field of creating intelligent machines	Technique where machines learn from data
Scope	Broad	Subset of AI
Main Goal	Simulate human intelligence	Learn patterns and make predictions
Learning	May or may not involve learning	Primarily based on learning from data
Data Dependency	May use rules, knowledge, or data	Usually requires training data
Decision Making	Can use rules, logic, search, and learning	Learns decision patterns from data
Examples	Expert systems, robotics, AI assistants	Spam detection, recommendation systems
Relationship	Contains ML as a subset	Subset of AI
Easy Way to Remember
AI = Making machines intelligent
             ↓
ML = Making machines learn from data
Example

Consider a self-driving car:

Artificial Intelligence
        │
        ├── Decision Making
        ├── Planning
        ├── Reasoning
        │
        └── Machine Learning
                │
                ├── Object Detection
                ├── Traffic Sign Recognition
                └── Prediction

AI represents the overall intelligence and decision-making system, while ML helps the vehicle learn from data and recognize patterns.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is the broader field of developing intelligent systems, whereas Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data. Thus, all Machine Learning is related to AI, but AI is not limited to Machine Learning.
